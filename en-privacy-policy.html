GPS Watermark Camera Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026.

Changsha Huoyubai Network Technology Co., Ltd. (长沙火羽白网络科技有限公司) operates GPS Watermark Camera for iPhone. This policy explains what the app processes, how the information is used and which choices you have.

Media and content you create

The app processes your camera feed, captured photos and videos, audio recorded with video, selected photos, watermark text and logos, QR code content, and editing inputs on the device. An optional baby profile can store details you enter, such as a name, birth date and related watermark fields. These files and inputs are not uploaded to our app servers as part of capture, editing, or QR export. If you save to Photos, share a file or use iCloud Photos or device backups, Apple or the recipient may store a copy according to your settings and their terms. A watermarked photo or video can reveal its location, time and any other information you chose to include.

Device permissions

  • Camera captures photos and videos.
  • Microphone records audio with videos when access is allowed.
  • Photos lets the app open selected images and save results to your photo library.
  • Location while using the app supports nearby places, addresses, coordinates, altitude, and weather watermarks. The app may ask for precise location where needed to improve a watermark’s accuracy.

In iOS Settings you can change these permissions. Features that rely on a permission may be unavailable when you turn it off.

Location, place search and weather

With your consent to the in-app location notice and permission from iOS, the app can prepare a location while it is in the foreground, before you take a photo. It can also get an updated location when you use a location-related feature. Our integration uses the AMap Open Platform iOS Location and Search SDKs, provided by 北京高德图强科技有限公司. AMap supports positioning, address lookup, nearby results, place searches and weather queries. It may process precise or approximate coordinates, terms you enter into place search, area or city information for weather, sensor data, app and version details, device model, operating system, screen resolution, carrier information, SDK identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information. The app uses the SDK builds without Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA). See the AMap Open Platform Privacy Policy.

The app can cache a recent position and a corresponding address on the device so a watermark can use recent data while an update is pending. You can withdraw AMap consent from Profile, then About, then Withdraw privacy consent. The app stops the associated requests, clears this cache and disables location watermarks until you enable the feature again. You can separately turn off iOS location permission. Withdrawal does not remove location information already printed on saved photos or videos.

Optional Apple sign-in and subscription status

Basic use, purchase and Restore Purchases do not require a GPS Watermark Camera account. If you choose Sign in with Apple, our account service receives the app-specific Apple user identifier and identity token to authenticate you. The app does not request your name or email address from Apple. The app can retain a local sign-in credential and a token used to associate a purchase with the app account. While signed in, the app may send the account identifier and App Store receipt to our account service for verification and membership synchronization. The service may process the associated product, transaction identifiers, entitlement status and expiration date.

Apple handles purchases and recurring payments. We do not receive your full payment card number or payment password. When you use the app without signing in, StoreKit verifies entitlement on the device and the app does not send an account-linked receipt to our account service. Apple’s services are governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Retention, deletion and privacy requests

Local works and settings remain on your device until you remove them or they are cleared. You can delete items from My Works and edit or remove baby profiles in the app. Separately delete copies you saved to Photos or shared elsewhere. We use account information to provide account access and membership synchronization. We keep it while needed for these services and may retain limited records when required by law or for resolving disputes.

If you signed in, you can request permanent deletion in Profile, then About, then Delete account. When deletion succeeds, the app clears the local sign-in session. Records that must be retained by law may remain for the required period. Follow Apple’s Sign in with Apple instructions to manage that authorization separately. Account deletion does not automatically delete local copies of your work or stop subscription renewal. See the Subscription Terms for cancellation instructions.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may be able to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to certain processing, or contact your privacy regulator. Use the email below to exercise your rights or report a privacy concern. Information you provide when contacting us is used to respond to your request.

Children and changes to this policy

The app is intended for a general audience. Only enter a child’s details or share media depicting a child when you have the necessary authority or consent. A parent or guardian can contact us about a child’s information. If our practices change, we will update this policy and provide notice or request consent where required.
