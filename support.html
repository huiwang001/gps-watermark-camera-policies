Support for GPS Watermark Camera, provided by Changsha Huoyubai Network Technology Co., Ltd. (长沙火羽白网络科技有限公司).

Email 439688809@qq.com for help with the app. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Remove private location or account details from any screenshots you attach.

Restore a subscription

  1. Check that the App Store is using the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  2. On the app’s home screen, open Profile, then Membership.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

For billing, see Apple’s cancellation instructions or how to request a refund from Apple.

Location missing from a watermark

  1. On the camera screen, tap Location and follow the location service prompt if it appears.
  2. In iPhone Settings, open Privacy & Security, then Location Services. Select GPS Watermark Camera and check that it has access while you use the app. Turn on Precise Location if you want to include precise coordinates.
  3. Open the watermark editor and check that the location or coordinates field is enabled. Check your network connection if nearby places, an address, or weather information is missing. Service availability varies by location.

You can change permissions in iPhone Location Services settings or withdraw AMap consent from Profile, then About, then Withdraw privacy consent.

Delete your optional app account

After signing in to the app, open Profile, then About, then Delete account. Follow the confirmation steps to delete the app account and its associated cloud data. Your App Store subscription is managed separately in Apple Account settings.
