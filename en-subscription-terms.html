Plans and benefits

GPS Watermark Camera offers a monthly auto-renewing subscription billed once per month and a yearly auto-renewing subscription billed once per year. An active subscription gives you unlimited use of watermarked photo and video capture, adding watermarks to photos, photo editing, stitching, splitting, and QR code export. The purchase screen describes the benefits that come with the selected plan.

Price and billing

Review the full amount billed for the selected period, your currency, renewal information and any offer conditions before confirming. Pricing may vary by App Store country or region. Apple charges your Apple Account when you confirm a purchase. If an introductory offer applies, review its duration, eligibility, and the amount that will be charged after the offer in Apple’s confirmation screen.

Renewal, cancellation and plan changes

Your subscription renews for another period of the same length unless you turn off auto-renewal. To avoid renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before your current period ends. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions and select GPS Watermark Camera. After cancellation, you generally keep access until the end of the paid period. Apple displays when a plan change takes effect and sends notices or requests your consent for applicable price increases. Apple’s cancellation instructions

Purchases without an app account

You can buy and restore a subscription without creating or signing in to a GPS Watermark Camera account. To restore an active subscription on a compatible device, use the Apple Account that made the purchase, then open Membership and tap Restore Purchases. Sign in with Apple is optional and can synchronize membership status to the app account. Signing out or deleting the app account leaves the App Store subscription in place. Use Apple’s subscription settings to stop renewal.

Billing questions and refunds

Apple handles payment and refund requests for App Store purchases. To request a refund, see Apple’s refund instructions. Eligibility varies by location and purchase. Your mandatory consumer rights still apply. If membership does not appear after purchase, verify that the correct Apple Account is active and try Restore Purchases.
